How it works

Build it, step in, live it.

Every block on the board can work: it has a body, state, actions and rules. When you want to know how the system feels, you don't press play — you step inside a character and try it.

BuildSketch like FigJam. Turn any block into an entity.
Step inYour gem takes a character. Its gem lights up.
LiveOnly what that body can do, from where it stands.
Step outWhat you lived is discarded. The design stays intact.
InspectEvery change, who caused it and why it was allowed.
AdjustChange the definition and step in again.

There is no play button. Only definitions. Nothing happens by itself: things change only when someone acts — you inside a character, or a machine reacting to it. The world is deterministic, so every outcome can be explained and replayed.

Entities with bodies

Weight, volume, hands, energy. Physics is enforced by the world, not by the action: nobody lifts a cash machine just because someone wrote "grab".

Characters you can enter

Each character is unique — its own synthetic iris and fingerprint, its own helmet. You choose from the same options a human, a script or an AI would see.

A workspace that is a place

Your Backyard holds your trees (folders) and their houses (files). It's a space too: walk up to a tree and go in.

Who it's for

For people designing experiences that mix the physical and the digital.

Service and product designers — and the teams they work with — use blueprints that don't run and screens with no world behind them. Problems show up at implementation. With Monoblock you build the branch, put a customer in it, step in, try to withdraw cash and find what breaks.

A living spec

The world you define is model, prototype and specification at once: entities, the actions they offer, the conditions and effects — readable by people, by engineers and by AI.

Local-first

Open it and use it, offline, on Android, iOS and the web. A sync server is optional — only for collaborating, sharing and publishing.

Built with Claude

AI that proposes. A kernel that decides.

Monoblock is being built with Claude Code — the simulation kernel, the physics and energy rules, identity, sync and the multiplatform interface. Inside the product, AI never writes the world directly.

  • Weaver next — describe a system in plain language; Claude proposes typed change sets (entities, actions, conditions, effects) that the kernel validates before applying.
  • Actor next — Claude plays characters by choosing from the same typed options a human sees, so every decision is explainable and replayable.
  • Agents next — terminal and MCP access, so external agents can author and test worlds.
  • Today now — the deterministic kernel, entity bodies, characters you can enter, Backyards, and an offline app on three platforms.
